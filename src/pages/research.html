Qwen 3.5 35B on an Apple M5: Larch vs Ollama and MLX

Qwen 3.5 35B on a 24 GB M5, from one to eight chats at once, against Ollama and MLX. One short prompt, one run at each count.

Tool-choice accuracy: three routing methods on two 8B models

Three routers for the same decision: the model alone, a logprob gate, a hidden-state probe. Wrong-tool rate over 200 prompts on two models.

Qwen3-8B chose the wrong tool 2.5 % of the time; Llama 3.1 8B, 24 %. The model mattered more than the router.
