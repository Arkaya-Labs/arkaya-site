OUR MISSION
{{SEAL}}

Intelligence should be owned, not rented.

Sui juris: in one's own right, under no one else's authority.

Capable open models exist, and many people already own a GPU that can run them. We build the software that connects the two, so AI runs on your own hardware, keeps your data there, and works offline without an account or subscription.

WHO WE ARE

The team

Who we are

We're three engineers. We wanted open models to run well on our own computers, found that existing inference engines weren't built with that hardware as the main target, and wrote our own.

We publish our code and our benchmark results, including the ones where we're behind.

Open source

Larch is open source under Apache 2.0, along with its benchmarks and the scripts that produced them. It was released on 26 November 2026.

HOW WE WORK

Engineering rules

These rules are in our repository and apply to every change, whether a person or an AI agent wrote it.

A skipped test is not a passing test.
We never weaken a test to make it pass.
Published numbers are never edited.
A person approves and merges every change.
Every claim needs a measurement behind it.
If we haven't measured it, we don't claim it.
